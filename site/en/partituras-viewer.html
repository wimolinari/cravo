Select a work · view in high resolution · perform at the instrument

Here are the scores edited by the UFRJ team — the eight Preludes and the Allemande by Couperin with annotated fingering, musical examples from Sancta Maria, and the original ornament tables. Use the zoom controls to view in any detail; the light theme is more legible for playing at the instrument.

★ How to use

Select a work below. The score opens in high resolution with zoom buttons (− 100% +) and full screen. To print, use your browser's option (Ctrl+P) — we recommend light mode for printing.

📖 Playing from the screen

To play directly from a computer or tablet screen:

  • Use full screen (⛶) to make the most of your display
  • Increase the zoom (+) until the notes are comfortable at your reading distance
  • Light mode offers better contrast for reading
  • To print: Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on Mac), preferably in A4 landscape

★ About the editions

The Couperin scores (Preludes and Allemande) and the Sancta Maria examples were typeset electronically by Maria Aida Barroso for the UFRJ 2013 edition. The ornament tables from Couperin (1713) and Rameau (1724) are facsimiles of the original editions. All rights reserved to the translators and editors.
